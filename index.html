The Legend of Zelda — Unity Recreation

We recreated the original Legend of Zelda dungeon experience in Unity, including combat, enemies, room transitions, and dungeon exploration.

Gold Level: Speed Tiles

Our original mechanic introduces two types of floor tiles: Fast Tiles and Slow Tiles. Fast Tiles increase the player's movement speed, while Slow Tiles decrease it.

Level Design

We designed four connected dungeon rooms that gradually introduce the new movement mechanic and increase the difficulty.

Room 1 — Introduction: Players encounter a slow zone and a fast zone, learning how movement speed changes before progressing to the next room.

Room 2 — Combat and Rewards: A fast pathway is surrounded by slow zones containing enemies, rupees, and hearts. Players must choose between faster navigation and collecting resources.

Room 3 — Advanced Challenge: The previous mechanics return with increased difficulty and Wallmasters acting as traps.

Room 4 — Final Challenge: The final room combines extensive fast and slow zones, requiring players to apply the movement strategies learned throughout the level.
