Project 2 — Ball Battle: Shrinking Arena

Ball Battle: Shrinking Arena is a single-player 2D survival game inspired by Agar.io and Ball Battle.

The player controls a ball inside a shrinking square arena and competes against computer-controlled enemy balls. Collect food to grow larger, consume smaller opponents, and survive until all enemies have been eliminated.

Core Mechanic

The arena gradually shrinks throughout the match, reducing the available space and increasing encounters between opponents.

Players must balance collecting food, avoiding stronger enemies, attacking smaller opponents, and staying inside the shrinking arena.

Difficulty Settings

Easy: 3 enemies, arena shrinks by 5 units every 30 seconds.

Medium: 5 enemies, arena shrinks by 10 units every 20 seconds.

Hard: 5 enemies, arena shrinks by 20 units every 10 seconds.

Enemy AI

The game includes five rule-based enemy AI personalities: Balanced, Aggressive, Defensive, Greedy, and Hunter.

Enemies can collect food, grow larger, chase smaller opponents, escape stronger opponents, and consume other enemies.

Additional Features

Real-time size-based leaderboard, randomized ball colors, difficulty selection, fullscreen settings, win and loss conditions, and a complete restart system.

Technologies

Unity 6, C#, Unity 2D Physics, Rule-Based Artificial Intelligence, Collision Detection, UI Development, and WebGL.

My Contributions

Designed and developed the game mechanics, player movement, food collection, ball consumption, enemy AI behaviors, dynamic arena shrinking, difficulty system, real-time leaderboard, user interface, and win/loss gameplay loop.

Controls

Move — Use the in-game movement controls. Collect food, grow larger, and consume smaller opponents while avoiding the shrinking arena boundary.
